Hi, I'm Hanne.

I'm a marketing consultant who has spent more than ten years on social media strategy, content creation and digital brand building.

I've looked after the European Commission's LinkedIn account and its 1.5 million followers, managed monthly ad budgets, and kept engagement growing steadily along the way.

I mix creative storytelling with analytical insight: data-driven content strategies, paid social campaigns and brand communities that feel like real ones. I work with brands, agencies and startups internationally.

What I do

Strategy and planning

Social media and content strategy, brand positioning, campaign planning, audience research, trend analysis and competitive intelligence.

Content and creative direction

Content design and curation, brand storytelling, visual identity, copywriting, concept development, and photo and video direction.

Performance and analytics

Tracking and reporting, A/B testing, conversion rate optimisation, ROI analysis, and setting the KPIs worth watching.

Client and project management

Client relationships, budgets, stakeholder communication, and coordinating teams and vendors from plan to delivery.
